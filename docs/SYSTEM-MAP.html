2 · One patient visit

LookCamera scans 61 signs every 2 s. Nothing alarming → carry on. A sign only becomes a question.
→
IntakeName · village · who it's for and age · consent. Anything already said is not asked again.
→
“What brings you in?”Claude restates it: “fever and chills for three days, is that right?”
→
The gateOnly what you SAID opens an area. At most two areas. A headache never opens Injury.
→
HistorySince when, always. “What can't you do” only if nothing opened. 1–10 only for pain.
Camera hunchesMax two signs. First confirm it in your words (“are you sweating a lot?”). A no drops it. A yes → its cause (“chest pain?”).
→
Area questionsUp to 4 per open area, danger signs first; physical tests where the camera can judge. 7 medical steps at most.
→
“Anything else?”Then, for every adult who consented: “would you volunteer for others in your village?”
→
Hard stop16 pod turns, whatever happened. A healthy check-up is 10; a cold 13; a headache 14.
→
Verdict + goodbyeWhat it most likely is, what is being sent, two self-care steps, the come-back-now signs.
EmergencyA red rule fired. Help is called; up to three danger questions; red goodbye.
LikelyA condition is confident: ≥70% with two findings (a benign one once the danger questions are no). Colour red/yellow/green. Exact order.
UncertainAn area opened, the questions ran out. The cover kit is sent anyway (fever: malaria test + paracetamol; stomach: ORS…). Worker checks today.
Nothing found“Nothing serious came up today.” Nothing ordered. Not everyone who comes in is sick.
Then: saved to the registry · handover note · order and opt-ins to SpacetimeDB → map, government, patients, community.

3 · What a red flag does

A rule firesOne-sided weakness · chest pressure + sweat · cannot breathe · heavy bleeding · child cannot drink / fit / limp · stiff neck + fever · pregnancy bleeding or fits · thoughts of suicide · SpO₂ < 90 · BP ≥ 160/110 · glucose < 70
→
“We are calling for help now”Said once. Intake and the rest are skipped.
→
Up to 3 danger questionsFrom that area: fits, bleeding, can they drink… Stroke runs the smile, arm and speech checks instead.
→
Red goodbye + orderPre-referral treatment is ordered (artesunate, ceftriaxone, blood, oxygen) so the health worker isn't waiting on a supply run.

5 · What the camera sees and what it triggers

10 pictures a secondRolling four seconds in memory. A face close-up every third picture.
→every 2 s
Burst of 8 to Jev61 yes/no signs over the burst. Answer in 0.2–0.6 s.
→
Two gatesFace/skin/eye signs need a close, lit, facing face. Posture signs (holding chest, cane, bandage) only need the body in view.
→≥ 60%
A hunch, not a verdictSmoothed over scans. One flicker reaches 40% at most.
Step 1: confirm the sign“I notice you look sweaty. Are you sweating a lot, even without heat?”
→no
DroppedYour word beats the camera. Never counts again.
→yes
Step 2: the cause“Do you have pain or pressure in your chest?”
→yes
Area opensChest / heart questions follow; a red rule can fire from the answers.
